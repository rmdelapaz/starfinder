The Starfinder Guide

Starfinder, from Paizo, is science-fantasy roleplaying: starships and laser rifles alongside magic and gods, in a galaxy that lost its own history and is busy finding it again. This guide covers the first edition of the Starfinder Roleplaying Game, from building a character through classes, themes and gear to running a campaign among the Pact Worlds, or playing alone.

Starfinder at a Glance

Four ideas carry most of the game. Each part of the guide builds on them.

The d20 Roll

Roll a d20, add your bonuses and meet or beat the Difficulty Class. Attacks roll against a target's Energy or Kinetic Armor Class.

Stamina, Hit Points, Resolve

Stamina absorbs damage first and comes back with rest; Hit Points are real injury; Resolve Points let you push on when it matters.

Race, Class, Theme

Your race says where you come from, your class what you do, and your theme the life you led before the adventure began.

Starships

The whole crew takes part in a starship fight: pilot, engineer, gunners, science officer and captain each have a job.
