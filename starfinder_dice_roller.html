Dice Roller

Checks, saves and attacks against a DC or Armor Class with exact odds, damage with critical hits, and a Stamina, Hit Point and Resolve tracker.

Set a bonus and a DC, then roll.

Exact Odds

A d20 has 20 equally likely faces, so every chance is a multiple of 5%. Each +1 to your bonus is worth 5%, until you can't fail (or can't succeed).

ResultChance

Damage

A critical hit rolls the damage twice and adds the results together, plus any critical hit effect the weapon has (such as burn or wound). Write dice as 1d6+1, 2d8, 3d6+4.

Stamina, Hit Points and Resolve

Stamina Points14
Hit Points12
Resolve Points4

Damage comes off Stamina first and the rest off Hit Points. At 0 HP you are dying: unconscious, losing 1 Resolve Point at the end of each of your turns, and dead if you would lose one you don't have. Stabilizing costs a quarter of your maximum Resolve (at least 1, at most 3); once stable, 1 RP at the start of your turn brings you back with 1 HP. After a 10-minute rest you can spend 1 RP to regain all your Stamina.
